### New

- **Time off.** Request annual leave, sick leave and other leave types from **Time off** in the sidebar (under **Team**). Ruby counts working days for you, skipping weekends and the public holidays HR has entered, and shows your balance before you send a request. Annual leave follows the usual 12 days a year, counted from your start date in your first year.
- **Approvals.** Your manager approves or rejects your requests; when you have no manager, HR or an admin does. Approvers see a count of requests waiting for them next to **Time off**.
- **Team calendar.** See who is away on which day. It shows names and half days only, never the reason or the leave type.
- **For HR.** Manage leave types, enter public holidays, adjust balances and see every change in the HR audit log.

### Improved

- **A tidier sidebar.** Your daily work sits at the top, the team pages under **Team**, and Search and Notifications are now small buttons next to the Ruby name. **What's new** and **Admin** moved to the bottom, above your account.
- **A fresh look.** Buttons, fields, menus and the board have a cleaner, more consistent design in light and dark mode.
