### New

- **Issues.** A new **Issues** page lists the tasks of every project in one place. Filter by type, status, priority, labels, assignee, reporter, project, milestone and dates, sort by last update, creation, priority or due date, and save the filters you use often as your own views. Select up to 100 issues on a page to change their status, priority, type, assignee, due date or team labels at once. On a phone, Issues is the first entry under **More**, and the filters open in a sheet.
- **Report an issue.** File a bug, a feature request or an improvement in a few seconds with **Report an issue** (the + button next to Search, or the first entry under **More** on a phone). It becomes a task in the project you choose, in Backlog, with you as the reporter.
- **Task types.** Every task is now a Task, a Bug, a Feature request or an Improvement. Change it on the task page; the type shows on the list, the board, My tasks and in search results. Existing tasks are Tasks.
- **Team labels.** Admins can create labels for the whole team under **Admin → Team labels**. Anyone can put them on a task in any project, next to the project's own labels. Team labels carry a small tag icon. A task can have up to 10 labels of both kinds together.
