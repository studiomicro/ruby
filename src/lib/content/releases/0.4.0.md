### New

- **Calendar.** Open **Calendar** in the sidebar (under **More** on your phone) to see what is happening by month, week or as an agenda. Your tasks with a due date, open milestones, team events, public holidays and who is out on approved leave are in one place. On a phone the agenda opens first; on a larger screen the month. Filters choose what to show: everyone's tasks or only yours, one project, completed items, or only the events you take part in.
- **Events.** Add a meeting or a deadline that is not a task: a title, a time or a whole day, a place, a Markdown description and attendees. An event can repeat daily, weekly or monthly until a date or a number of times, and you can remove one occurrence or the whole series. Times are in Jakarta time (WIB).
- **Private events.** A **Private** event is visible only to you and the people you invite. Everyone else, admins included, does not see it.
- **Reminders.** Ask Ruby to remind you before a task is due (on the day at 09:00, or 1 day, 2 days or a week before, or at a time you choose), before an event starts, or at any time for a note of your own. Reminders arrive in **Notifications** and are visible only to you. Manage them on **My reminders**, which you can reach from the calendar and from your account page.
- **E-mail reminders.** Once e-mail is set up for Ruby, you can turn on e-mail for your reminders on your account page. Until then, reminders arrive in the app only.
