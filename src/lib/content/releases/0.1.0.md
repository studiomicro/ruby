- **Invite-only accounts.** There is no public sign-up. An admin invites each person by e-mail and sets their role.
- **Admin area.** Admins manage people, roles and invitations, and can unlock or disable an account. Ruby always keeps at least one active admin.
- **Works on your phone.** A tab bar at the bottom on phones, a sidebar on larger screens, and a layout that keeps clear of the notch and the home indicator.
- **Safe by default.** Sign-in locks after repeated failures, sessions end when you sign out, and Markdown text is shown safely.
