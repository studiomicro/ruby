### New

- **Install Ruby.** You can now add Ruby to your home screen and open it like an app, in its own window. On iPhone, tap **Share** in Safari, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**. On Android and in desktop Chrome or Edge, use **Install Ruby** on your account page or in the **More** menu on your phone.
- **Offline page.** If your signal drops, Ruby shows a clear "You are offline" page instead of a blank screen, and a slim notice at the bottom of the screen while you are offline. Nothing is saved while you are offline: a form you send keeps what you typed and tells you so, so try again when you are back online.
- **Refresh.** The installed app has no browser reload button, so **Refresh** in the **More** menu and on your account page loads the latest data without losing what you typed. In the installed app, pages that are not one of the four tabs also get a back button in the top bar.
- **Updates.** When a new version is ready, the update banner appears in the installed app too, and **Reload** switches to it.
- **Smoother Ruby.** Sheets, menus and dialogs now slide and fade in, cards glide to their new place on the board, and new comments and saved rows are easy to spot. On phones, moving between pages fades. If you turn on "Reduce Motion" on your device, Ruby shows none of it.

### Privacy

- Ruby keeps only its own app files on your device, never your pages, tasks or people data. Signing out clears everything Ruby stored on your phone.
